# Shep's Katana Legend

A 3D samurai sword-fighting game for tablets. Swipe to slash, parry at the gold ring, chop off armor and limbs, win your opponent's sword, forge rare blades, train your skills, and duel friends.

Play: https://tonyashive-max.github.io/katana-legend/

Tip: on an iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to play full screen.
