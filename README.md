# Shep's Katana Legend

A 3D samurai sword-fighting game for tablets.

**Play:** https://tonyashive-max.github.io/katana-legend/

- Roam four lands, find hidden spirit orbs, solve shrine puzzles, and defeat each land's warriors.
- Open the spirit gate to face a mythical beast: Akaoni the Ogre, the Frost Giant, the Tengu King, and Ryujin the Sky Dragon.
- Swipe to slash, parry at the gold ring, hold BLOCK for red-ring attacks, and watch your stamina.
- Chop off armor and limbs (no blood), win your opponent's blade, forge it stronger, and train your skills in the dojo.
- Duel a friend on the same tablet, or on two devices with a 4-letter code.

Tip: on an iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to play full screen.
